## 1. Role Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
The Worker/Employee role is for regular staff members who need to track their attendance, view personal records, and submit requests. This is the most common role in the system.

### 1.2 Responsibilities
- Clock in and out daily
- Maintain accurate attendance records
- View personal payroll and attendance
- Submit overtime requests (if applicable)
- Keep personal information updated
- Respond to notifications

### 1.3 Who Uses This Role
- Construction workers
- Office staff
- General employees
- Support personnel
- Any staff not in supervisory/engineering roles

## 2. Access & Permissions

### 2.1 What You Can Access

| Module | Access | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Personal Dashboard** | Full | Your attendance, basic info |
| **Clock In/Out** | Full | Record your daily attendance |
| **My Profile** | View/Edit | Update your information |
| **My Payroll** | View Only | View your pay records |
| **My Attendance** | View Only | View your attendance history |
| **Overtime Requests** | Submit Only | Request OT (if eligible) |
| **Settings** | Personal | Password, photo, notifications |

### 2.2 What You Cannot Access

**You CANNOT:**
- View other employees' records
- Mark attendance for others
- Access payroll calculations
- Approve any requests
- Edit system settings
- View company-wide reports
- Access admin functions

### 2.3 Your Privacy
- Your payroll information is private
- Only you, Admin, and Super Admin can see your data
- Your personal information is protected
- Activity logs track access for security

## 3. Getting Started

### 3.1 First Login

**Steps:**
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your **Employee Code** (given by HR)
3. Enter your **temporary password** (given by HR)
4. Click "Log In"

**Password Change Required:**
1. System will ask you to change password
2. Enter current (temporary) password
3. Enter new password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Confirm new password
5. Click "Update Password"

### 3.2 Setting Up Your Profile

**Navigate to:** Settings or Profile page

**Recommended Setup:**
1. **Upload Profile Photo:**
   - Click "Upload Photo"
   - Select clear photo of yourself
   - Crop if needed
   - Save

2. **Update Contact Information:**
   - Verify email address
   - Update phone number if available
   - Add emergency contact (if field available)

3. **Enable Notifications:**
   - Go to Settings
   - Click "Enable Notifications"
   - Allow browser to send notifications
   - Test notification

### 3.3 Getting Your QR Code

**Your QR Code:**
- Generated automatically when account created
- Used for clocking in/out
- Can be printed or kept on phone

**Where to Find:**
1. Ask HR/Admin to show you
2. View in your profile (if available)
3. Print for your ID badge

## 4. Daily Attendance

### 4.1 Clocking In (Morning)

**Important:** Clock in when you arrive at work site

**Method 1: QR Code Scan (Recommended)**
1. Open the system on your phone or station
2. Go to employee portal
3. Click "Scan QR Code"
4. Allow camera access if asked
5. Hold your QR code in front of camera
6. Wait for beep and confirmation message
7. Verify time shown is correct

**Method 2: Manual Selection**
1. Go to employee portal
2. Find your name
3. Click "Clock In"
4. Confirm your location
5. Wait for confirmation

**What to Check:**
- Time displayed is correct
- Location/branch is correct
- You see "Clock In Successful" message

### 4.2 Clocking Out (End of Day)

**Important:** Clock out when you finish work

**Process:**
1. Same as clock in
2. Scan QR code again OR click "Clock Out"
3. Verify time-out recorded
4. Confirm message appears

**Troubleshooting:**
- If system says "Not clocked in" - Contact Admin
- If time is wrong - Note actual time, tell Admin
- If location wrong - Tell Admin correct site

### 4.3 Checking Your Attendance Status

**Daily Check:**
1. Log into system
2. View your dashboard
3. Check today's status:
   - 🟢 **Present** - Recorded successfully
   - 🟡 **Late** - Recorded but after start time
   - ⚪ **No Status** - Not yet recorded

**If Status is Wrong:**
- Contact Admin immediately
- Explain what happened
- Request correction

### 4.4 Common Attendance Issues

| Problem | What to Do |
|---------|------------|
| Forgot QR card | Contact supervisor, request manual entry |
| Phone battery died | Ask to use station device, or request manual entry |
| System won't accept clock-in | Check internet, try again, or contact Admin |
| Wrong site showing | Tell Admin, they can correct it |
| "Already clocked in" error | You may have already recorded - check with Admin |

## 5. Personal Information

### 5.1 Viewing Your Profile

**Navigate to:** Settings or Profile

**Information Shown:**
- Employee Code
- Full Name
- Position/Role
- Branch/Site assignment
- Daily Rate
- Contact Information
- Profile Photo

### 5.2 Updating Your Information

**You Can Update:**
- Profile photo
- Email address
- Phone number (if field available)
- Password

**You CANNOT Update:**
- Employee Code
- Position (contact HR)
- Daily Rate (contact HR)
- Branch assignment (request transfer)

**How to Update:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Click field you want to change
3. Enter new information
4. Click "Save" or "Update"
5. Wait for confirmation

### 5.3 Changing Your Password

**Steps:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Change Password"
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password (min 8 characters)
5. Re-enter new password to confirm
6. Click "Update Password"
7. Log out and log back in with new password

**Password Tips:**
- Use letters and numbers
- Don't share with others
- Change if you think someone knows it
- Remember it or keep in safe place

## 6. Requests & Approvals

### 6.1 Requesting Overtime (If Eligible)

**When to Request:**
- When asked to work extra hours
- Before working overtime
- As soon as you know you'll work OT

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to Overtime section
2. Click "Request Overtime"
3. Fill the form:
   - Date of overtime
   - Expected hours
   - Reason (who asked you, what project)
4. Submit request
5. Wait for approval

**Important:**
- Don't work OT without approval (may not be paid)
- Request in advance when possible
- Emergency OT: Tell supervisor immediately

**Checking Status:**
- 🟡 Pending - Waiting for approval
- 🟢 Approved - Will be paid
- 🔴 Rejected - Will not be paid (see reason)

### 6.2 Requesting Leave (If System Available)

**Types of Leave:**
- Sick Leave - When you're sick
- Vacation Leave - Planned time off
- Emergency Leave - Unexpected situations

**Process:**
1. Go to Leave section
2. Select leave type
3. Choose dates
4. Provide reason
5. Attach doctor's note if sick > 2 days
6. Submit request
7. Wait for approval

**Before Taking Leave:**
- Request must be approved first
- Emergency: Call supervisor, request later

### 6.3 Requesting Site Transfer

**When to Request:**
- Assigned to different project
- Need to work at another site
- Permanent or temporary move

**Steps:**
1. Contact your supervisor first
2. If approved, go to Transfer section
3. Fill transfer request:
   - Current site
   - Requested site
   - Date
   - Reason
4. Submit
5. Wait for approval

**After Approval:**
- Clock out at old site
- Go to new site
- Clock in at new site

## 7. Viewing Your Records

### 7.1 Viewing Your Attendance History

**Navigate to:** Attendance or Reports section

**What You'll See:**
- Calendar view or list
- Each day marked:
  - Green = Present
  - Red = Absent
  - Yellow = Late
  - Blue = Leave
- Time in and time out

**Checking Specific Dates:**
1. Select month/year
2. Find date
3. Click to see details

**If You See a Mistake:**
- Note the date and error
- Contact Admin immediately
- Request correction

### 7.2 Viewing Your Payroll

**Navigate to:** Payroll or Reports section

**Monthly Payroll Shows:**
- Days you worked
- Your daily rate
- Basic pay (days × rate)
- Overtime pay (if any)
- Allowances (if any)
- Deductions:
  - SSS
  - PhilHealth
  - Pag-IBIG
  - Cash Advance (if you have loan)
- Net Pay (what you receive)

**Understanding Deductions:**
- **SSS:** Social Security (for retirement, sickness, etc.)
- **PhilHealth:** Health insurance
- **Pag-IBIG:** Housing and savings
- These are required by Philippine law
- Taken automatically from payroll

**If You Have Questions:**
- Ask Payroll Admin
- Show them your report
- Point out specific concern

### 7.3 Cash Advance Balance (If Applicable)

**If You Have Cash Advance:**
1. Go to Cash Advance section
2. View your balance
3. See payment history
4. Check deductions from payroll

**Important:**
- Cash advance is deducted from pay
- Keep track of your balance
- Pay off as soon as possible

## 8. Support & Help

### 8.1 When to Contact Admin

**Contact Admin For:**
- Attendance corrections
- Password reset
- Payroll questions
- System not working
- Profile updates

**How to Contact:**
- Through system message (if available)
- Email: [Admin email]
- In person at admin office
- Through your supervisor

### 8.2 Common Questions

**Q: I forgot to clock in, what do I do?**
A: Tell your supervisor immediately. They can ask Admin to mark you present. Do this same day if possible.

**Q: My pay is wrong, what do I do?**
A: Get your payroll report, check the numbers, then contact Payroll Admin with specific details.

**Q: I can't log in.**
A: Check you're using correct employee code and password. If still can't, ask Admin to reset password.

**Q: How do I see how much leave I have?**
A: Go to Leave section. Your available balance should be displayed.

**Q: Can I change my branch?**
A: Request through supervisor. They will process transfer request.

**Q: What if I'm at a different site today?**
A: Clock in at whatever site you're at. System should detect location. Tell Admin if wrong site shows.

### 8.3 Important Reminders

**Daily:**
- ☐ Clock in when you arrive
- ☐ Clock out when you leave
- ☐ Check that times are correct

**Weekly:**
- ☐ Review your attendance for the week
- ☐ Check for any errors
- ☐ Report problems to Admin

**Monthly:**
- ☐ View your payroll report
- ☐ Verify all days accounted for
- ☐ Check deductions are correct
- ☐ Report any issues before payday

### 8.4 Emergency Situations

**If System Is Down:**
1. Write down your time in on paper
2. Write down your time out on paper
3. Give to supervisor
4. Admin will enter when system is back

**If You Have Emergency:**
1. Notify supervisor immediately
2. Follow company emergency procedures
3. Attendance can be fixed later
